Leafy & Me privacy policy

Yours, by nature.

Your plants, your collection, your private iCloud.

Last updated: 25 September 2026 · iPhone and iPad

No separate account. No advertising. No analytics.
Your collection is stored on your device and, when you enable private iCloud sync, in your Apple Account’s private iCloud storage. You can also export a backup to a location you choose.

Leafy & Me is a plant care app. Visit leafyandme.com for information and support. This policy covers Leafy & Me for iPhone and iPad, including private iCloud sync.

Your collection

Leafy & Me stores your plant names, plant types, room labels, light conditions, photos, care schedules, and history in its private storage on your device. Room labels are text you enter; Leafy & Me does not request your device’s geographic location. The app does not send your collection to us or a developer-operated server. When you enable private iCloud sync, Apple synchronizes your collection using your account’s private CloudKit database. This requires an Apple Account and iCloud availability. Your collection remains available on your device offline; syncing changes requires a connection.

Private iCloud sync keeps changes, including deletions, up to date across your devices; it is not a separate historical backup. You can export a snapshot to iCloud Drive using Export Collection. Your device’s own backups may also include app data according to your Apple backup settings. Apple handles iCloud services under its privacy policy.

Permissions and reminders

Camera access is requested only when you choose Take Photo. You can select a photo with Apple’s system picker instead, use a bundled reference image, or keep no photo. Leafy & Me receives only the library items you select through the picker.

Notifications are optional. They are scheduled locally to remind you about care. Leafy & Me stores reminder preferences and occurrence identifiers locally to avoid repeating alerts. You can revoke camera permission and notifications in iOS Settings and continue using the app.

Retention and deletion

Your collection remains on your device and, when synced, in your private iCloud storage until you delete it. Swipe a plant in the Plants list and confirm Delete Plant to remove its photo, rules, and history. Leafy & Me retains record identifiers and deletion dates to prevent deleted records from reappearing when older data is merged. These markers do not retain the deleted photo, plant name, or care history. With sync enabled, deletions propagate to other devices when they connect.

Deleting Leafy & Me removes its local app storage; offloading the app is different and can preserve that storage. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove its iCloud data. Manage iCloud storage and device backups through your Apple Account settings. Before enabling iCloud, Leafy & Me also saves a recovery snapshot in its private app storage; this remains separate from later changes to your collection. There is no separate Leafy & Me user account to delete.

Backups you export

Export Collection creates a JSON file containing your collection and photos. Leafy & Me does not encrypt this file. You choose its destination through Apple’s file interface, which may include cloud providers. Protect copies from unauthorized access and delete them separately from Files or the chosen destination. Deleting a plant or uninstalling Leafy & Me does not delete exported files.

Tracking and external pages

Leafy & Me includes no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking, or automatic developer diagnostics. Its plant catalog and reference photos are bundled and work offline.

Opening support, policy, source, or license links opens a website outside Leafy & Me. The receiving site processes that visit under its own privacy practices; Leafy & Me does not send your collection with the link. If you email support, your email address, message, and any attachments are handled by the maintainer and email service providers to respond to your request; they are not automatically collected by the app. If you choose GitHub for support, the information you submit is handled by GitHub. Issues and any attachments are public. Review GitHub’s privacy statement before submitting personal information.

This website

This website contains no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, contact forms, or embedded third-party content. Fonts use your device’s system fonts, and styles and images are served from this site. The site does not set cookies or use browser storage.

The hosting provider receives information needed to serve a page, such as your IP address and browser request details, and may retain server logs under its own practices. Following an external link, including GitHub support links, takes you to a separate website governed by that website’s privacy practices.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or help managing your data, contact Leafy & Me at support@leafyandme.com. You may also choose to use public GitHub issues. Do not post private photos, collection backups, passwords, or other sensitive information. A GitHub account is required only if you choose to create an issue.

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date. If a future version changes data handling, its policy and App Store disclosures will be reviewed before release.
