## Catalog names and care suggestions

The app’s {{ site.catalog_count }} catalog entries use plant names documented in retail listings from [Intratuin](https://www.intratuin.nl/kamerplanten), [123planten](https://www.123planten.nl/kamerplanten), [Fleurdirect](https://www.fleurdirect.nl/kamerplanten/), and [PLNTS](https://plnts.com/nl/shop/all-plnts). These are dated references for plant selection, not a guarantee of current stock or an authoritative botanical classification. Retailer descriptions and photographs are not redistributed.

Care suggestions draw on the [RHS houseplant growing guide](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/types/houseplants/growing-guide) and [Intratuin’s houseplant care guidance](https://www.intratuin.nl/inspiratie/kamerplanten-verzorgen). Numeric care intervals are Leafy & Me’s editorial starting points, not instructions to water automatically. Check your plant and soil, then adapt the schedule to your conditions.

Reference photographs may show a related species or genus rather than the exact species or cultivar. They are illustrations, not automatic plant identification or a guarantee of a cultivar match. Full per-entry references are available in the app’s **Settings → Catalog → Sources**.

## Reference photographs and screenshot credits

The app’s reference library contains 206 photographs, reused across catalog entries. The credits below cover the reference photographs, including the plant thumbnails shown in the homepage’s App Store screenshots. Credits are reproduced from the application catalog at commit `b24fba2`; individual photographs retain their creators’ rights and licenses.

The bundled images were resized without upscaling, JPEG-compressed, stripped of camera metadata, and displayed with square crops in the app. The website screenshots are resized and compressed reproductions of the [App Store images]({{ site.app_store_url }}). CC BY-SA photographs, including their resized and cropped versions, remain under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) with no additional restrictions. CC0 photographs are dedicated to the public domain.

For the three larger photographs used directly on this website, see the [website photo credits]({{ '/credits/' | relative_url }}).
