Thoughtfully simple

Care reminders.
Room to grow.

From your first windowsill friend to a home full of green. Everything you need to build a care routine that feels natural.

Your plant collection, organized.

Give every plant a name, a room, and a photo. Find it again by name, type, or room, whether you have three plants or thirty.

Plant care schedules that fit.

Choose two preferred care days to bring nearby checks together. Adjust intervals and seasonal schedules to suit each plant.

One daily care reminder.

One daily summary brings your care tasks together. Unfinished care stays visible, without the same reminder over and over.

Watering, feeding, and more.

Keep track of feeding, repotting, rotating, and trimming, too. Add custom tasks for the things that make your plants yours.

A care history for every plant.

See what you did and when. Record care, snooze a check, correct a date, or undo an accidental tap. Still moist? Check again later.

A backup you can keep.

Export your plants, photos, and care history to Files. Save a copy to iCloud Drive or another location you choose, and import it when moving to another device.

Find your kind of green

An offline plant catalog.
Right at home.

Explore 560 catalog entries, with care suggestions to help you get started. Find a match for your light, room temperature, humidity, and preferred care frequency.

  • Browse the catalog and reference photos offline
  • Start with suggested care, then make it your own
  • Add a custom plant type whenever you need to

Care suggestions are starting points. Always check your plant and soil before watering. Some reference photos show a related species or genus.

Yours, by nature

Your collection.
Right here with you.

Your plants, photos, schedules, and care history are stored on your device. Leafy & Me works offline and does not automatically sync between devices. Export a backup to Files when you want a separate copy or to move your collection. No separate account, advertising, or analytics.
