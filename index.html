Set up your plant
care routine.

01

Meet your plant.

Pick a plant from the catalog or add your own type. Give it a name, a spot in your home, and a photo.

02

Find your rhythm.

Review the suggested schedules, choose your two preferred care days, and turn on reminders if you like.

03

Let good habits grow.

Open Care to see what needs attention. Check your plants, record what you did, and enjoy watching them grow.

Yours, by nature

Your collection.
Right here with you.

Your plants, photos, schedules, and care history are stored on your device. Leafy & Me works offline and does not automatically sync between devices. Export a backup to Files when you want a separate copy or to move your collection. No separate account, advertising, or analytics.
